# The v2.3.1 outline is 98.0 x 77.16 mm, grown 12 mm away from the fingers

A bench measurement on a CoCo 3 showed the v2.3 98 x 55 mm board put the module's USB-C port on the case surface and the reserved HDMI corner only 10 mm past it. v2.3.1 grew the body 12 mm away from the fingers (67.0 mm body + 10.16 mm finger tongue). This is a bare board that fits no Program Pak shell; the cased variant is a separate future design. Details: `docs/hardware-design.md` §6 and §9.
