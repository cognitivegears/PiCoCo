================================================================
PiCoCo — PCB Fabrication Checklist (main board, v2.3.1)
READ THIS BEFORE SUBMITTING TO ANY BOARD HOUSE
================================================================

The cartridge edge fingers insert into a 40-year-old CoCo slot. Using
JLCPCB's or PCBWay's default plating options WILL destroy the slot's
bronze wipers over time. The settings below are not optional.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
JLCPCB ORDER SETTINGS (v2.3.1, 2026-09)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

  Base material FR-4 / Layers 2 / Dimensions 98 x 77.2 mm overall (67.0 mm body + 10.2 mm finger tongue) / Thickness 1.6 mm (critical)
  Surface finish: ENIG (all pads gold; fingers get hard gold below, never HASL)
  Outer copper 1 oz
  Gold fingers: YES (critical) / Bevel: 30 deg (critical) / Gold thickness: 1 um min (2 um better)
  Order remark: Gold fingers / 30 deg bevel on the protruding bottom tongue edge only (52.8 mm
  wide), not the full board edge.
  Mark on PCB: "Remove Mark" (no order-number placeholder on the board since v2.3.1)
  Castellated holes: No / Impedance: No
  SMT assembly: Top side, Economic. Files: fab/main/PiCoCo-BOM-jlc.csv, fab/main/PiCoCo-CPL-jlc.csv
  Extended parts on this board: SN74LVC245A x4 (one part number), SN74LVC00A x1. Confirm stock.
  Parts NOT placed (by design): U1 module (yours), R2 R10 R15 R17 Q3 C12 C15 J1 (DNP). Q4/R16/R18
  (the JP5-reachable /CART drive stage) ARE placed regardless of module.
  JP2 ships bridged 1-2 (hardware /OE default); JP3 ships bridged 1-2, which now routes
  AUDIO_PWM to header pin 34 by default (Pico 2 sound out of the box) — cut 1-2/bridge 2-3 to
  put E on header pin 34 instead; JP4 (Q_CART<->CART_CART autostart tie) ships open by default;
  JP5 (header-pin-34 /CART or /NMI drive select) ships open by default and shares that header
  pin with JP3 — never bridge both JP3 and JP5. J1 ("EXP (Plus-W)" header) is DNP.
  Check the placement preview: every SOIC/SOT pin-1 dot matches the silkscreen. If any part is
  rotated, fix the ROT table in tools/jlc_post.py, rerun tools/gen_fab.sh, re-upload.
  Look hardest at D2 (SMA diode: cathode band must face U1/VSYS, reversed = board never powers)
  and U14 (SOT-223 AMS1117: tab/pin 2 is +3V3 output; reversed = +5V on the 3.3V rail and every
  logic IC dies). JLCPCB's SMA and SOT-223 zero angles often differ from KiCad's by 180 deg.
  Unverified LCSC numbers, confirm value/voltage in the parts step: C17710 (470R, R19/R20),
  C1710 (10nF), C17513 (1k), C17477 (0R, R24), C28323 (1uF, C15). C45783 (C3, 22uF 25V) was
  verified 2026-09-17 and is no longer on this list. The rest were checked 2026-09-17.
BUDGET VARIANT (bare boards, hobbyist): same Gerbers, ENIG, gold fingers NO, bevel NO.
  ENIG fingers wear after tens of insertions; fine for a lightly used cart.
PLUS-W INSTALL: fab/main/stencil-module/*F_Paste.gbr is a paste stencil for the 15 grid pads
  only — the 40 castellation apertures sit over plated holes and paste falls through them, so
  hand-solder the castellations (hot air or a paste syringe for the 15 grid pads); or mount on
  2x20 headers and forgo the 15 pads.
FIT (measured 2026-09-18 on a CoCo 3): opening 114 x 30 mm, connector face 43 mm inside the case,
  connector slot depth ~6 mm, so the case surface is ~49 mm from the finger tip; the shoulders
  (10.2 mm from the tip) never touch the connector and the USB-C port is ~16 mm outside the case.
MODULE: solder the Pico 2 flat on the castellations, or on 2x20 headers. Nothing under it.
  Pico 2 flat mount: its three underside SWD/debug pads sit on the carrier's GP29/GP32/GP35
  grid pads (they overlap by ~0.05 mm). Put Kapton tape over those three grid pads first, or
  mount the Pico 2 on headers. Not an issue for the Plus-W, which uses the grid.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THESE SETTINGS MATTER
-----------------------------------------------------------------

* Hard gold plating on fingers: the CoCo slot uses bronze wipers.
  HASL or ENIG plating will abrade within tens of insertion cycles,
  leaving oxide that ruins signal integrity on /CTS, /SCS, and data.
  Hard gold lasts thousands of cycles.

* 30° bevel on leading edge: a square-cut edge will scrape and lift
  pad material on insertion. The bevel glides into the slot.

* 1 µm gold minimum: thinner plating wears through quickly,
  especially on older CoCo slots where the wipers may already be
  worn to an abrasive surface.

* 1.6 mm board (= 0.062"): the CoCo slot is spec'd for 0.062"
  boards. 1.2 mm is too thin (loose fit, intermittent contact).
  2.0 mm is too thick and won't insert.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
POST-ORDER VERIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------

When boards arrive:
1. Verify edge fingers are plated gold, not silver-tinted (HASL).
2. Verify 30° bevel is present (feel with a fingernail — should
   slope, not square).
3. Test-fit into a CoCo slot BEFORE soldering anything. If the
   board doesn't seat, a light sand on the bevel usually fixes it.

If a board was shipped with HASL fingers or no bevel, CONTACT THE
FAB HOUSE for a replacement — do not insert it into a real CoCo.
