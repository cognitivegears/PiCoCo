================================================================
PiCoCo — PCB Fabrication Checklist (main board, v2.3)
READ THIS BEFORE SUBMITTING TO ANY BOARD HOUSE
================================================================

The cartridge edge fingers insert into a 40-year-old CoCo slot. Using
JLCPCB's or PCBWay's default plating options WILL destroy the slot's
bronze wipers over time. The settings below are not optional.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
JLCPCB ORDER SETTINGS (v2.3, 2026-09)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

  Base material FR-4 / Layers 2 / Dimensions 98 x 55 mm / Thickness 1.6 mm (critical)
  Surface finish: ENIG (all pads gold; fingers get hard gold below, never HASL)
  Outer copper 1 oz
  Gold fingers: YES (critical) / Bevel: 30 deg (critical) / Gold thickness: 1 um min (2 um better)
  Remove order number: "Specify a location" (the JLCJLCJLCJLC text on the bottom silkscreen)
  Castellated holes: No / Impedance: No
  SMT assembly: Top side, Economic. Files: fab/main/PiCoCo-BOM-jlc.csv, fab/main/PiCoCo-CPL-jlc.csv
  Extended parts on this board: SN74LVC245A x4 (one part number), SN74LVC00A x1. Confirm stock.
  Parts NOT placed (by design): U1 module (yours), R2 R3 C12 C15 Q3 Q4 R15 R16 R17 R18 (DNP).
  Check the placement preview: every SOIC/SOT pin-1 dot matches the silkscreen. If any part is
  rotated, fix the ROT table in tools/jlc_post.py, rerun tools/gen_fab.sh, re-upload.
BUDGET VARIANT (bare boards, hobbyist): same Gerbers, ENIG, gold fingers NO, bevel NO.
  ENIG fingers wear after tens of insertions; fine for a lightly used cart.
PLUS-W INSTALL: fab/main/stencil-module/*F_Paste.gbr is a paste stencil for the 55 module pads
  (15 hidden). Hot air or a paste syringe; or mount on 2x20 headers and forgo the 15 pads.
MODULE: solder the Pico 2 flat on the castellations, or on 2x20 headers. Nothing under it.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
WHY THESE SETTINGS MATTER
-----------------------------------------------------------------

* Hard gold plating on fingers: the CoCo slot uses bronze wipers.
  HASL or ENIG plating will abrade within tens of insertion cycles,
  leaving oxide that ruins signal integrity on /CTS, /SCS, and data.
  Hard gold lasts thousands of cycles.

* 30° bevel on leading edge: a square-cut edge will scrape and lift
  pad material on insertion. The bevel glides into the slot.

* 1 µm gold minimum: thinner plating wears through quickly,
  especially on older CoCo slots where the wipers may already be
  worn to an abrasive surface.

* 1.6 mm board (= 0.062"): the CoCo slot is spec'd for 0.062"
  boards. 1.2 mm is too thin (loose fit, intermittent contact).
  2.0 mm is too thick and won't insert.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
POST-ORDER VERIFICATION
-----------------------------------------------------------------

When boards arrive:
1. Verify edge fingers are plated gold, not silver-tinted (HASL).
2. Verify 30° bevel is present (feel with a fingernail — should
   slope, not square).
3. Test-fit into a CoCo slot BEFORE soldering anything. If the
   board doesn't seat, a light sand on the bevel usually fixes it.

If a board was shipped with HASL fingers or no bevel, CONTACT THE
FAB HOUSE for a replacement — do not insert it into a real CoCo.
